# Demand verdict

As of 23 Oct 2026. Live version with comments: [Claude Doc](https://claude.ai/artifact/Nu1bZKGvLdYX6wF9s9QdaG)

## Verdict

Demand is real, but narrower than the PRD assumes. Lead with appointment-only, high-ticket businesses in Goa, not general salons.

- **The pain is proven.** Zenoti and Fresha both built automatic waitlist refill. Zenoti reports $780K recovered from 18K refills in 2024.
- **India numbers are missing.** No published Goa or India no-show data. The first 15 owner conversations are the market research.
- **Salons are a weak first niche.** Panaji haircuts average about ₹275–500, walk-ins fill gaps for free, and Fresha's waitlist is free.
- **Better first niche:** skin and aesthetic clinics, physio, nail and lash studios, then dental. Appointment-only, ₹1,000–10,000 a slot, booked on WhatsApp, no refill tool.
- **Customers:** 3–5 paid pilots in 30–45 days is realistic with in-person visits. 10 paying needs about 60–100 conversations.

## Evidence

- 47% of beauty and wellness businesses get short-notice cancellations or no-shows every week; 62% of cancelling clients give under 24 hours' notice; about 7% of monthly revenue is lost ([Fresha, Dec 2025, UK](https://www.fresha.com/blog/cancellations-cost-study)).
- 44% of owners say repeated no-shows hurt their mental health (same source).
- India caveat: many salons run on walk-ins, which backfill gaps for free. Appointment-only businesses don't have that cushion.

## Willingness to pay

- ₹999/month per location works where one slot is worth ₹1,000+.
- WhatsApp utility messages cost about ₹0.145 each in India ([AiSensy, Oct 2026](https://m.aisensy.com/blog/whatsapp-pricing-update-october-2026/)), so messaging cost is tiny.
- Pilot offer: free 2-week setup, then ₹999/month only if it fills 3+ slots.

## Market figures used in the deck

- India beauty and personal care market about $28B, growing 10–11% a year; branded chains are only 10–15% ([Franchise India, Sep 2026](https://www.franchiseindia.com/insights/en/article/top-10-beauty-salon-franchises-tapping-indias-rising-beauty-spend.59666)).
- About 1.89 lakh registered dentists in India, 397 in Goa (CBHI via [India Data Map](https://indiadatamap.com/2025/08/01/map-which-state-have-highest-number-of-dentists-2025/); national figure is our sum of the state table).
