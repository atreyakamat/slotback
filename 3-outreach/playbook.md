# Weekend GTM playbook

Live version with comments: [Claude Doc](https://claude.ai/artifact/SxT1b4WkHrhwUqYhVqd2TE)

Lead with a Goa market study on how pre-booked businesses run. Offer SlotBack only if they ask.

**Three rules**

- **Make the study real.** Compile the answers and send every participant the Goa report. It's your reason to reach out, your thank-you, and your reason to follow up.
- **Be honest when asked.** If someone asks "are you building something?", say yes in one line. You don't pitch, but you don't hide it.
- **Weekend goal:** 20+ verified leads, 10+ study answers, 5+ meetings for Mon–Wed, 1–2 owners asking to see the product.

## The flow

1. **DM** on WhatsApp, email or Instagram, inviting them into the study (10 min, or a 2-minute form). Participants get the report and a free trial (15 days to a month, their pick).
2. **They reply:** book a slot within 48 hours, in person if possible.
3. **Meeting (15 min):** run the study questions, listen, take notes. No product talk.
4. **The turn:** share one insight and the efficiency guide. Then stop.
5. **Branch:**
    - They ask what you're building: 2-minute demo, then set up their free trial. Stage = Opportunity.
    - They don't ask: thank them, promise the report. Stage = Met.
6. **Report day:** send the study report to everyone. It ends with: "We're testing a small tool for the biggest gap we found. Reply if you want to try it."

## How to verify a lead

A lead is verified when all five are confirmed by phone, visit or reply:

- [ ] Open and real: the number answers or the shop is open.
- [ ] Decision maker: you know who runs the schedule and who decides on tools.
- [ ] Pre-booked: most business comes from appointments, not walk-ins.
- [ ] Booking method: WhatsApp, phone, register, or which software.
- [ ] Best contact: the owner's preferred channel and time.

Fastest way: ask the front desk "Who handles your appointments, and what's the best time to catch them?"

## The 12 study questions

Short form = questions 1–6 and 12. Ask about last week, not "usually".

1. What type of business is it, and roughly how many appointments a week?
2. How do clients book? (WhatsApp, phone, walk-in, Instagram, website, app: which one?)
3. Who manages the schedule day to day?
4. Last week, how many clients cancelled with under 24 hours' notice, or didn't show up?
5. Average value of one appointment? (Under ₹500 / ₹500–1,000 / ₹1,000–3,000 / over ₹3,000)
6. When a slot opened at short notice, what did you do? (Nothing / called or messaged clients / posted on Status / it filled itself)
7. How long did that take, and how often does it work?
8. Do you keep a waiting list of clients who wanted an earlier slot? Where?
9. Do you send reminders, take deposits or charge cancellation fees? How has that gone?
10. Which part of running bookings takes the most staff time?
11. If you could fix one thing about your schedule, what would it be?
12. Can I send you the Goa report? Best WhatsApp number or email.

## Call script (15 min)

You talk about 20% of the time. Write their exact words for questions 4, 5 and 6.

**1. Open (1 min)**

> Thanks for making time. I'm talking to pre-booked businesses across Goa about how the day really runs: bookings, cancellations, no-shows. There are no right answers, and I'm not here to sell you anything. Everyone who takes part gets the full report. Okay if I take notes?

**2. The study (10 min).** Questions 1–11. Follow-ups when an answer is short: "Tell me about the last time that happened." "What did that cost you, roughly?" "Who had to deal with it?" "What have you tried?"

**3. The turn (2 min)**

> Something interesting so far: most places I've spoken to get a few short-notice cancellations a week, and the ones that fill them fastest keep a list of clients who wanted an earlier slot. I put together a one-page guide on booking efficiency; can I send it to you?

Then stop talking. Count to five.

**4a. If they ask what you're building**

> I'm building a simple tool for exactly this. When someone cancels, it messages clients on your waiting list, and the first to tap gets the slot. Can I show you on my phone? It takes two minutes.

After the demo:

> Your free trial is ready whenever you are: 15 days, 20 days or a month, you pick. I set everything up. After that it's ₹999 a month only if you want to keep it. Which length works for you?

**4b. If they don't ask**

> That's everything. Thank you, this really helps. I'll send you the guide today and the report when it's done. Is this WhatsApp number the best one?

**5. Same day:** send the guide, log notes in the sheet, set Stage to Met or Opportunity.

## Objection handling

**During the study**

| They say | You say |
| --- | --- |
| "We're busy right now." | "Totally understand. Would 10 minutes on Monday work, or can I leave the 2-minute form?" |
| "Are you selling something?" | "Not today. I'm doing this study first. I am building a tool for one of the problems, and I'm happy to show you if you're curious, but that's not why I'm here." |
| "We don't have cancellations." | "That's great, and useful to know. What do you do differently?" |
| "Talk to the doctor/owner." | "Of course. When's the best time to catch them, and is there a number I should message?" |
| "Why do you need this?" | "I'm mapping how Goa's pre-booked businesses run so I can share what works. Your name stays out of the report; I only share combined numbers." |
| "Send me something on WhatsApp." | "Sure. I'll send the 2-minute form now. Okay if I check back Tuesday?" |

**After the demo**

| They say | You say |
| --- | --- |
| "We already have software." | "Which one? Does it offer the open slot to your waiting list automatically?" If yes (Fresha, Zenoti), step back. If no, it runs alongside. |
| "We just call people ourselves." | "How long does that take, and how often does it work? This sends one message and the first to tap gets it." |
| "My clients won't use links." | "It's a normal WhatsApp message with one button. Let's try it on five of your regulars." |
| "₹999 is too much." | "Fair. Try it free first, up to a month. What's one empty slot worth to you? If it doesn't pay for itself, you just stop." |
| "I'm worried about spamming my clients." | "Only clients who join your waiting list get messages, with a limit per opening. You see every message before it's turned on." |
| "Let me think about it." | "Of course. What would you need to see to say yes?" Then book a follow-up date. |
