| # | Slide |
| --- | --- |

| 10 | How we'll do it |

**Edit and re-render:** change `source/deck.html`, then run `NODE_PATH=<global node_modules> node source/render.js` from this folder. It uses the brand fonts and logo in `../brand/`.

Growth numbers on slide 9 are targets, not results. Market figures and their sources are on slides 2, 6 and 7.
