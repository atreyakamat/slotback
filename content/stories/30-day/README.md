# SlotBack 30-day Story plan

One Story a day, 26 Oct to 24 Nov 2026. Post around 10:00 IST. The images are in `content/stories/30-day/` (day-01.png … day-30.png) and in the project files.

**Arc:** Week 1 teases the product, Week 2 names the problem, Week 3 shows how it works, and Week 4 builds up to demos opening on Day 30.

**Engagement:** every Story with "Vote below ↓", "Slide it ↓", "Tell us below ↓" or similar has empty space at the bottom for an Instagram sticker. Add the sticker in the app. Sticker replies count as interactions, and they're what keeps your Stories at the front of the tray.
